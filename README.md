# Mild Stimulation IVF: Is the Gentler Protocol Right for You?

A short guide to Mild IVF (also called Mini IVF or low-dose IVF): how it works, its costs, and who it suits.

> ✍️ **Author:** Dr. Krupa A. Shah, Ayuh Fertility Centre, Ahmedabad
> ⚠️ This guide is for general education only. Your specialist will choose the protocol that fits your case.

---

## What Is Mild Stimulation IVF?

Mild IVF uses **lower doses of fertility medicines** (or tablets such as Letrozole or Clomiphene) over a shorter period. The aim is **2–6 good-quality eggs** instead of the 8–15+ that conventional IVF aims for.

**Quality over quantity**, with fewer injections, fewer side effects and less strain on your body.

> It's different from **natural cycle IVF**, which uses no stimulation and retrieves a single egg.

---

## How It Works

1. **Evaluation:** AMH, follicle count, hormone and thyroid tests, and a semen analysis
2. **Low-dose stimulation:** Starts on Day 2–3 of your cycle and lasts about 7–10 days
3. **Monitoring:** Usually 2–3 ultrasound scans
4. **Egg retrieval:** Under light sedation, 34–36 hours after the trigger injection
5. **Fertilisation:** IVF or ICSI in the lab
6. **Transfer:** On Day 3 or Day 5. Extra embryos can be frozen.

---

## Mild vs. Conventional IVF

| Factor | Mild IVF | Conventional IVF |
|---|---|---|
| Medicine dose | Lower, or partly oral | Full dose |
| Injection days | 7–10 | 10–14 |
| Eggs retrieved | 2–6 | 8–15+ |
| OHSS risk | Much lower | Higher |
| Discomfort | Less | More |
| Procedure cost* | ₹80,000–₹1,10,000 | ₹1,20,000–₹1,40,000 |
| Total with medicines* | ₹1,00,000–₹1,40,000 | ₹1,60,000–₹2,20,000 |
| Success per transfer | Comparable | Comparable |
| Cycles needed | May need more | Fewer |

*Approximate costs per cycle at Ayuh Fertility Centre.

> 💡 **Key point:** Success per embryo is similar. The main difference is **how many embryos you get per cycle**.

---

## ✅ Benefits

- Fewer injections and less medicine
- Much lower risk of OHSS
- Less bloating and discomfort
- Lower medicine cost
- Easier to manage emotionally

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Fewer embryos to select, test (PGT-A) or freeze
- May need more cycles
- Not suitable for everyone

---

## Who Is It For?

| ✅ Good fit | ❌ Usually not ideal |
|---|---|
| PCOS with very high AMH | Women over 37–38 |
| Past severe OHSS | Those needing many embryos for PGT-A |
| Women sensitive to hormones | Poor responders |
| Younger women with good egg reserve | Couples who can't manage several cycles |

**Low AMH?** This is decided case by case, based on your age, past response and goals.

---

## Myths vs. Facts

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Mini IVF always has lower success | Results per embryo are comparable |
| More injections = better results | Not true, and the OHSS risk goes up |
| It suits everyone | Whether it suits you is a medical decision |
| IVF medicines cause permanent harm | No credible evidence of this |

---

## FAQ

**Is Mild IVF cheaper?**
Per cycle, yes. If you need more cycles, the total cost may end up similar or higher.

**How do doctors decide?**
They look at your age, AMH and follicle count, past response, diagnosis and personal goals.
